A quick primer: what a moon sign is

In Western astrology, your moon sign is the zodiac sign the Moon was in at the time you were born. Because the Moon moves through a sign in roughly two and a half days, working it out accurately needs your birth date and ideally your birth time and place. Astrologers usually associate the moon sign with emotional needs and instinctive reactions, as opposed to the sun sign, which is the sign most people know from horoscope columns. You can look up your moon sign for free with many online birth-chart calculators before paying for an interpretation.

How to judge any paid astrology reading

  • Know what you are getting: a generated report, or a reading written by a named person.
  • Check whether it asks for your birth time. A reading that only uses your date of birth is necessarily more general.
  • Read the price and any order-page add-ons carefully, and keep your receipt in case you want a refund.
  • Treat it as reflection or entertainment, not advice.
