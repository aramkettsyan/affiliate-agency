Looking into Pianoforall? This review keeps it honest and to the point: what it actually is, who it's a good fit for, and what to weigh before you buy.

What is Pianoforall?

A long-running, well-reviewed self-paced piano course that teaches chords and playing by ear instead of dense sheet-music theory.

What's in the course (per the official page)

As of November 2026, the vendor's official page describes Pianoforall as 10 ebooks with video and audio lessons built in. You get them two ways: online access to the "Pianoforall Academy" course player, which keeps lessons in order and saves your progress, and ebooks you download that work offline once saved. Everything is delivered digitally. Nothing physical is shipped.

The page lists these sections:

  • Rhythm-style piano: popular rhythms, ballad patterns, tricks and "bluffs"
  • Blues and rock 'n' roll rhythms
  • Chords, including inversions and advanced chords
  • Ballad style, with improvising and melodies
  • Jazz piano and advanced blues piano
  • Classical pieces, with reading sheet music alongside playing by ear
  • "Speed learning": scale and arpeggio exercises in every key

The page also mentions a student community and email support from the course creator. Some "Classics By Ear" courses are sold separately, so check what's included before you buy.

Price

When we checked in November 2026, the official page advertised a 7-day free trial or a one-time payment of $49, plus a 60-day money-back guarantee. The page doesn't say whether the trial needs a card. Prices change, so confirm on the official page.

Who is it best for?

Pianoforall is best for adult beginners who want to play songs quickly without years of formal lessons. If that matches what you're after, it's worth a closer look.

The good

  • Aimed at a clear audience: see "Who is it best for?" above
  • 60-day money-back guarantee through ClickBank
  • Secure checkout handled by ClickBank

Things to keep in mind

  • Sales pages use marketing language, so weigh the claims with healthy skepticism
  • Price and offers can change, so confirm current details on the official page
  • Some upsells are offered after purchase

The bottom line

Pianoforall is a solid option in the learn piano and keyboard at home space for the right person. Because it's sold through ClickBank, it's backed by a 60-day money-back guarantee, so trying it is low-risk. Check current pricing and full details on the official page below.
