Common cat questions this kind of guide helps with

Most "what is my cat telling me?" questions come down to reading sounds, posture and context together. These are the situations our readers ask about most, each with a free, practical guide of its own:

  • Why does my cat meow at night? — energy, routine and learned attention-seeking, plus when night yowling means a vet visit.
  • Why does my cat bite me while petting? — the tail, ear and skin-twitch warnings that come before an overstimulation bite.
  • Why does my cat headbutt me? — scent marking and social bonding.
  • Why is my cat suddenly clingy? — changes at home, stress, or a possible health issue.
  • Why does my cat knock things off tables? — play, hunting instinct and attention.

A structured guide to cat communication is most useful as background for questions like these: it helps you notice patterns instead of guessing at each behavior on its own. It is not a substitute for a vet when a behavior change is sudden.
