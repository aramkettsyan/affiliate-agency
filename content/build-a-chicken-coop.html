What to plan for before you build any coop

Whichever plans you use, a few design decisions matter more than the exact board sizes. These are general points to check against any set of coop plans, not claims about what this particular guide contains:

  • Space per bird. Size the coop and run for the number of hens you actually expect to keep, with room to grow. Our guide on how much space chickens need walks through the usual rules of thumb.
  • Nesting boxes. You need fewer boxes than hens; see how many nesting boxes you need.
  • Ventilation without drafts. Vents high on the walls, solid walls at roost height. This matters most in winter, as covered in keeping a coop warm without electricity.
  • A sheltered, sunny spot for water. If the coop will have no power, plan a wind-protected, sun-facing corner for the waterer. See how to keep chicken water from freezing without electricity.
  • Local rules. Check zoning, setbacks and any limits on flock size before you buy materials.
