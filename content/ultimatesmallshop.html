What is UltimateSmallShop?

UltimateSmallShop (sold as the "Ultimate $1000 Small Shop") is a downloadable guide for setting up a home woodworking workshop in a small space on a limited budget. The sales page credits it to Ralph Chapman, who describes himself as a woodworker with more than 25 years of experience who coaches other hobbyists. The central promise is a complete shop for under $1,000, with a tool shopping list and floor plans for spaces as small as 8'x8'.

According to the official page, it is a 246-page illustrated book split into six modules:

  • Tool selection — deciding which tools you actually need before you start buying, and how to avoid low-quality tools.
  • Space selection — choosing where to put a long-term shop.
  • Shop layouts — floor plans and machine-placement recommendations for small or oddly shaped spaces, including making stationary machines mobile.
  • Electricity, lighting and sound proofing — planning power and lighting, whether to DIY or hire a pro, and reducing noise (including a list of lower-noise tools).
  • Heating, cooling, ventilation and dust — keeping the shop usable across seasons and managing dust.
  • Safety and more — organizing the shop and keeping it safe.

The page also lists two bonuses with the order: "The Workshop Cheat List" (tips on buying lumber and tools at a discount) and a "Deal Alert Service" that sends notices about tool deals.

Who is it best for?

It fits beginner and hobbyist woodworkers who are starting a shop from scratch, or squeezing one into a garage bay, shed, or basement corner, and who want a structured plan for what to buy and where to put it. The heating and ventilation module is relevant if you work in an unheated garage; our guide on gluing wood in a cold garage covers why that matters for glue and finishes.

It is less useful if you already have an established, well-equipped shop, or if you are looking for project plans rather than shop-setup advice.

The good

  • Covers the whole setup process in one place: tools, space, layout, power, climate, and safety.
  • Focused on small spaces and tight budgets, which is where most beginners actually are.
  • Delivered as a digital download; the official page says everything can be downloaded right after purchase.
  • The official page states a 60-day money-back guarantee, and the order is processed through ClickBank.

Things to keep in mind

  • It's a guide, not tools. The "under $1,000" figure is for the tools you'd still need to buy separately.
  • The sales page leans on strong marketing claims, such as large savings from a "secret tool source." Treat those as the vendor's claims, not guarantees, and compare prices yourself.
  • Tool recommendations reflect the author's personal opinions, and specific models and prices date quickly.
  • When we checked, the page showed a $29 price against a stated $79 retail and called it a limited-time offer. Confirm the current price at checkout.

The bottom line

UltimateSmallShop is a reasonable, low-cost starting point if you're planning a first woodworking shop in a small space and want a single structured guide to tools, layout, and shop systems. It won't replace hands-on experience, and you should sanity-check any tool recommendation against current reviews. With a stated 60-day refund window, the risk of trying it is low.
