Looking into EZ Battery Reconditioning? This review keeps it honest and to the point: what it actually is, who it's a good fit for, and what to weigh before you buy.

What is EZ Battery Reconditioning?

A step-by-step course that teaches how to recondition old, worn-out batteries (car, phone, laptop) back to usable working capacity instead of throwing them out.

According to the official sales page (checked November 2026), the program is a set of step-by-step guides with pictures and diagrams showing how to recondition different types of batteries, using supplies the vendor says most people already have at home. The battery types mentioned on the page include car batteries, laptop and cell phone batteries, drill and camera batteries, and AA/AAA cells. The page does not publish a module list or table of contents, so we can't tell you exactly how much space each battery type gets.

Format, price and guarantee

  • Format: digital. The official page describes downloading the guides after payment and viewing them online, including on a phone or tablet. It is not described as a video course.
  • Price: the official page lists it at $47 as a one-time payment, with no monthly fees. Prices can change, so confirm on the official page before buying.
  • Guarantee: the page states a 60-day money-back guarantee, with payment processed by ClickBank.
  • Extras: the page mentions lifetime updates on a members-only page, lifetime support, and a free "information vault" of articles. Bonuses are mentioned but not itemized.

Who is it best for?

EZ Battery Reconditioning is best for budget-conscious DIYers who want to extend battery life and cut down on replacement costs. If you're comfortable working carefully with tools and following written instructions, it's worth a closer look. If you just need your car to start tomorrow morning, a charge, a free load test at an auto parts store, or a new battery is the faster route.

The good

  • Aimed at a clear audience: hands-on DIYers who would rather fix than replace
  • Covers several common battery types, not just car batteries
  • One-time price and a 60-day money-back guarantee through ClickBank
  • Secure checkout handled by ClickBank

Things to keep in mind

  • Sales pages use marketing language, so weigh the claims with healthy skepticism. The page's headline promises a return to full working condition, while its own FAQ concedes the program won't bring batteries back "magically". Expect results to vary with the battery's age and condition.
  • Reconditioning can't fix physical damage: a cracked case, a dead cell, or a swollen or leaking battery should be recycled, not reconditioned.
  • We found no safety guidance on the sales page itself. Car batteries contain sulfuric acid and can release flammable hydrogen gas, and damaged lithium batteries can be a fire risk, so wear eye protection and gloves, work in a ventilated area, and never charge a frozen, swollen or leaking battery.
  • Price and offers can change, and some upsells may be offered after purchase, so confirm current details on the official page.

The bottom line

EZ Battery Reconditioning is a reasonable, low-cost option in the battery reconditioning / home savings space for the right person: a careful DIYer with a few tired batteries who wants to learn the process. It won't rescue every battery, and it doesn't replace basic safety precautions. Because it's sold through ClickBank with a 60-day money-back guarantee, trying it is low-risk.
