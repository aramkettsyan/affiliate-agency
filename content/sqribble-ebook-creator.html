What is Sqribble?

A drag-and-drop ebook design tool that turns text into formatted PDF ebooks, reports, and lead magnets using pre-built templates, aimed at marketers and course creators.

Who is it best for?

Sqribble is best for solopreneurs and marketers who need polished ebooks or lead magnets without hiring a designer. If that matches what you're after, it's worth a closer look.
